Ombuntu

The Omarchy Hyprland desktop on Xubuntu / Ubuntu 26.04.

On a fresh Ubuntu or Xubuntu 26.04, run:

curl -fsSL https://ombuntu.org/install.sh | bash

Then log out and choose Ombuntu at the login screen.
